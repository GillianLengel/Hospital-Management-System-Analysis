# Hospital Management System Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores hospital operational data to examine appointment performance, medical records, departmental staffing, and bed resources.

Using Python, Pandas, SQL, and Power BI, I performed exploratory data analysis (EDA), prepared and examined the dataset, identified operational patterns, and developed an interactive dashboard to communicate the findings.

The project demonstrates my ability to work with structured data, investigate business questions, identify trends, and present analytical results through interactive visualizations.

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Examine hospital appointment activity and outcomes.
- Analyze appointment completion, cancellation, and no-show patterns across departments.
- Explore medical record distributions.
- Compare staffing levels across hospital departments.
- Examine bed distribution and average patient length of stay by ward.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to communicate key findings.

## Project Workflow

The project followed a structured analytical workflow:

1. **Data Inspection** — Examined the hospital dataset, table structures, columns, and data types.
2. **Data Preparation & Cleaning** — Investigated data quality, missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis** — Used Python, Pandas, and SQL to explore hospital operations and examine patterns within the data.
4. **Analytical Exploration** — Compared appointment outcomes, staffing distributions, medical records, and bed resources.
5. **Data Modeling & Visualization** — Organized the data in Power BI, established table relationships, and developed DAX calculations.
6. **Dashboard Development** — Created an interactive three-page report to communicate the analytical findings.

## Key Analytical Areas

The analysis focused on several areas of hospital operations:

- **Appointment Performance:** Examined appointment outcomes and departmental no-show rates.
- **Medical Records:** Explored the distribution of medical records across departments.
- **Staffing Distribution:** Compared the number of doctors, nurses, and helpers across departments.
- **Bed Resources:** Examined the distribution of listed beds across hospital wards.
- **Length of Stay:** Compared average patient length of stay across wards.
- **Admissions Analysis:** Explored the relationship between recorded admission volume and average length of stay.

These analyses are descriptive and provide insight into patterns within the dataset. They do not independently establish operational efficiency, staffing adequacy, or patient outcomes.
